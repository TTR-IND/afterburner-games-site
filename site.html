Who We Are

A new studio with an old-school instinct

Technyx Interactive is a brand new game studio founded by Welsh game developer Josh Wheatstone.

We are not chasing graphical arms races or claiming that newer hardware automatically makes better games. Our technology exists to serve the game. Fun, responsiveness, engine efficiency, and a distinctive feeling are the priorities.

The goal is to understand what made older console games memorable — their pacing, limitations, visual identity, mechanics, and personality — then expand on those ideas in meaningful ways rather than simply imitating them.

Design Position

Principles & influences

Games that leave room to breathe

Difficulty and tension are valuable, but players need space. Systems should allow for exploration, management, or simply existing in the game world. Not every minute requires objectives, notifications, or reward loops.

Games should be enjoyable to spend time in.

No microtransactions

If you buy a game, you get the game. No virtual currencies, loot boxes, battle passes, or content withheld to be sold later. Releases are payware or donationware. That is the entire relationship.

Mechanical Sympathy

Design around hardware. Prioritise cache, bandwidth and allocation strategy over clever algorithms.

Browser as Platform

Useful constraints on memory and distribution. Instant play without installers or large downloads.

Character over Spectacle

Chunky geometry, distinctive animation, compact loops. Technical compromises that become personality.

Welcome to the Street

Dope Wars - King of the Street is the ultimate street empire simulator. You start with a small wad of cash and a dream. Buy product, avoid the loan-sharks, and flip your way to the top. But watch out — the cops are always watching. Do you have what it takes to become the King of the Street?

A reimagined classic concept with responsive controls and tight economic gameplay. Every day is a new opportunity. Every deal could be your last.

#strategy #action #economy #street #retro #simulation #dope-wars

How to Play

Master the street economy with these simple rules:

  • Buy Low: Check the market prices and buy when product is cheap.
  • Sell High: Prices fluctuate when you travel. Sell when you see a profit.
  • Manage Inventory: Don't get into debt or the loan-shark may destroy your plot.
  • Avoid the Cops: Random police raids can wipe your inventory. Stay alert.
  • Build Your Rep: Levelling up unlocks buildings and locations.
  • Goal: Turn $1,000 into $1,000,000 to become the King.

Pro Tip: Buy when prices are in the green, sell in the red. Always keep some cash in reserve.
