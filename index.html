About

What we're building now

Afterburner Games is deep in development of the Axiom3D Game Engine — a browser-capable 3D foundation built for performance and character. The engine has accidentally spawned its own flagship title: Redline Rally: Revolution, a brand-new rally game featuring military-grade vehicle physics, designed to be genuinely enjoyable and inspired by PS2 classics like V-Rally and Colin McRae Rally.

We prioritise solid engineering, readable systems, and games that feel as good as they look. No filler, just pure driving and technology.

Engine-first approach

Axiom3D is the core. It is built to handle simulation, rendering, and vehicle dynamics without unnecessary bloat. The rally game is both a testbed and a passion project — the physics are military-grade but tuned for fun.

The design language remains rooted in the late 90s / early 2000s: compact game loops, deliberate memory usage, and gameplay that respects the player.

Principles

How we work

Games that leave room to breathe

Games can be difficult and tense. They should also give the player space. Sit down, manage something, explore, or simply mess around. Tension works better when recovery exists. Not every minute needs notifications, objectives and reward loops.

Games should be enjoyable to spend time in.

No microtransactions

If you buy a game, you get the game. No virtual currencies, loot boxes, battle passes, or content deliberately withheld to be sold later. Releases are payware or donationware. That is the entire relationship.

Mechanical Sympathy

Design around hardware. Cache, bandwidth and allocation strategy before clever algorithms.

Browser as Platform

Useful constraints on memory and distribution. Instant play without installers or large downloads.

Character over Spectacle

Chunky geometry, distinctive animation, compact loops. Technical compromises that become personality.
